# Chapters 2 and 3 mapping-notation audit

In `8JU9`, $p_t$ maps $B\setminus\Phi$ bijectively onto $\Phi$. Translation by $((1,1),t)$ changes the first quadratic bit from $1$ to $0$, and translation by its additive inverse recovers each input. The §2.2 introduction states the bijection, and the proof of `8JVD` cites it where the sum is reindexed. The weight function in `8JU9` is arbitrary. The Fourier expansion in `8JVD` applies to any such function that satisfies its existing upper bound, and `8JVK` supplies the particular weight functions used by the chain.

The audit covered all 9 introductions, 27 statements, and 17 proofs in the two chapters.
